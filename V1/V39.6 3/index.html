My story

Hey! I'm Krish, a third-year CSE student with an AIML specialisation at Bennett University, from Noida.

I work across web development and AI/ML, from computer vision and neural networks to LLM apps with LangChain and GenAI. I've taken part in a few hackathons and built a handful of minor and major projects along the way.

I did a three-month summer internship at Mankind Pharma as an SDE working on AI/LLM projects. I also build open-source tools, like claude context shift and leetpush, and have solved 180+ LeetCode problems.
